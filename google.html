Our Philosophy

Roasted weekly. Poured with mindful intent.

We believe that extraordinary coffee needs very little interference. Our roasting profile highlights bright florals, sweet stone fruit, and the crisp terroirs of our producing partners.

01 /

Micro-Batch Roasting

Every bean is roasted on our vintage cast-iron drum roaster in batches no larger than 8 kilograms to ensure absolute precision, temperature control, and repeatable sweetness.

02 /

Transparent Origin

We pay an average of 140% above Fair Trade minimums. We know the farmers by name and track each harvest from seed bed, to wet mill, to our port.

03 /

Dialed To Season

Because beans are seasonal agricultural produce, our espresso and filter lineup rotates quarterly to showcase what is at its absolute peak flavor freshness.
