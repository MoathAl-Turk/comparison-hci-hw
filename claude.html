Roasted here, in small batches

We buy directly from growers we know and roast in batches of ten kilos or less. Every bag is dated on the day it leaves the roaster, so what you take home is always fresh.

Our house espresso is medium roast with notes of cocoa and toasted hazelnut. Single-origin beans rotate each month.

Whole beans are sold by the bag at the counter. We’ll grind them to suit your brewer.
